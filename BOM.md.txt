# 🖨️ Printed Parts

You'll need to print a substantial number of parts from the official STL files on Printables. The Configurator will generate a printed parts list tailored to your configuration. Make sure to print with a well-calibrated printer and the recommended filament (e.g., ABS/ASA/PETG for structural parts).
